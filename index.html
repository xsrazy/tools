Why everything runs in your browser

Most tool sites send your input to a server. That is slow, and it means your text, passwords and numbers travel across the internet. Every tool here does its work locally with JavaScript: what you type never leaves the page.

The side effect is speed. Counts, conversions and generations update on every keystroke with no round trip.

Built to be useful

  • Transparent math: calculators show the exact formula they use, so you can check the work.
  • No accounts: no sign-up, no tracking of what you type.
  • Educational, not advice: calculators give planning estimates. Big decisions deserve a licensed professional.
