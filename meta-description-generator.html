How the generator works

Give it your page title and the keywords you want to rank for, then pick a tone. The generator weaves them into a search-friendly sentence, then trims the result at a word boundary so it lands near 155 characters without cutting a word in half. The counter turns green when the length sits in the 120-160 sweet spot and amber when it is too short or too long.

Tips for better meta descriptions

  • Front-load keywords: Google bolds query terms in your snippet, so matching words stand out.
  • Add a reason to click: a benefit, a number or a question beats a plain summary.
  • Stay unique: write one description per page, never copy the same text everywhere.
  • Match the page: the snippet should describe what the visitor actually finds after clicking.

Meta description generator FAQs

How long should a meta description be?
Aim for 120 to 160 characters. Google typically shows about 155 to 160 characters on desktop before cutting the snippet with an ellipsis.
How does this generator write the description?
It combines your page title, target keywords and chosen tone into a search-friendly snippet, then trims it at a word boundary so no word is cut in half.
Do meta descriptions affect rankings?
They are not a direct ranking factor, but a clear, keyword-rich snippet raises click-through rate, which brings more traffic from the same rankings.
Should I include keywords in the meta description?
Yes. Google bolds the searcher's query terms inside your snippet, so matching keywords make your result stand out and confirm relevance.
Is my input uploaded anywhere?
No. The description is generated in your browser with JavaScript. Your text never leaves your device.
