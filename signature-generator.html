How it works

Type your name and the tool renders it in 100 handwritten styles using cursive fonts on your device. Click the style you like, adjust ink color and size, then download a transparent PNG you can drop onto any document.

The PNG exports at double resolution, so the signature stays crisp when printed or zoomed.

Signature generator FAQs

Is my name uploaded anywhere?
No. The signature is drawn on a canvas in your browser. Your name never leaves your device.
Does the downloaded signature have a transparent background?
Yes. The PNG is exported with full transparency, so you can place it over documents, photos or letterheads.
Is this signature legally binding?
This tool creates a signature image for visual use. Whether an image signature is legally valid depends on your country and the document type, so check local rules for contracts.
Why does my signature look different on another device?
The styles use handwriting fonts installed on each device. If a device lacks a font, the browser falls back to its default cursive font, which can look slightly different.
Can I change the ink color?
Yes. Use the ink color picker for classic blue or black ink, or any color you like, and adjust the stroke size with the slider.
