How to make a meme

1. Upload a photo (drag and drop works) or pick a built-in template. 2. Type your top and bottom captions. 3. Adjust the font size and hit Download PNG.

Captions use the classic meme style: bold white text with a black outline, centered on the image. Keep lines short for the biggest impact.

Tips for better memes

  • Contrast: the black outline keeps text readable on any photo.
  • Brevity: two short lines beat one long paragraph.
  • Timing: templates are 800 by 800, perfect for social feeds.

Meme generator FAQs

Is my uploaded photo sent to a server?
No. Your photo is loaded into the page with a local file reader and drawn on a canvas in your browser. It never leaves your device.
What image formats can I upload?
Any format your browser can display: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP and most others. The meme is always downloaded as a PNG.
Can I make a meme without uploading a photo?
Yes. Pick one of the four built-in template backgrounds and add your captions on top of it.
What size is the downloaded meme?
Uploaded photos keep their own dimensions, capped at 1200 pixels on the longest side for fast rendering. Templates render at 800 by 800 pixels.
Can I use multiple lines of text?
The top and bottom fields are single-line for the classic look. Keep each caption short and let the outline do the work.
