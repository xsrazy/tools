Why convert WebP

WebP is a modern format that keeps files small, but some editors, printers and older tools still cannot open it. PNG opens everywhere and keeps every pixel with zero loss, while JPG trades a little quality for much smaller files. This converter reads your WebP in the browser, draws it to a canvas, and exports it in the format you choose.

Which format should you pick

  • PNG for screenshots, graphics with text, and anything you will edit again.
  • JPG for photos you want to share or upload with a small file size.
  • 92% quality is the sweet spot for JPG: visibly identical to the original at normal viewing.
  • Resolution is preserved: width and height never change, only the container.

The converted file downloads automatically after conversion. Nothing is uploaded at any point.

WebP converter FAQs

How do I convert WebP to PNG?
Drop or pick a WebP file, choose PNG as the output format, and press Convert and download. The PNG downloads automatically.
How do I convert WebP to JPG?
Choose JPG as the output format and set the quality slider. JPG output is usually much smaller than PNG, which is handy for sharing.
Does converting lose image quality?
PNG output is lossless: zero quality loss. JPG output loses a tiny amount depending on the quality slider, though 92% looks identical in normal viewing.
Are my images uploaded anywhere?
No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser using the canvas API. Your image never leaves your device.
Can I convert multiple files at once?
This tool converts one image at a time. Drop a new file to convert the next one; each conversion downloads separately.
