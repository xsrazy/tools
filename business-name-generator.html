How the names are built

Each industry has curated prefix and suffix lists. The generator combines them with your keyword in three patterns: prefix + keyword, keyword + suffix, and prefix + keyword + suffix. Duplicates are removed and you get a fresh shuffled set every time.

Picking a winner

  • Say it out loud: if you have to spell it twice, drop it.
  • Shorter is better for domains, logos and social handles.
  • Avoid names that box you into one product if you plan to expand.
  • Always check the .com and your local trademark database before launch.

Business Name Generator FAQs

How does the business name generator work?
It combines your keyword with curated prefix and suffix lists for the industry you pick, in three patterns: prefix plus keyword, keyword plus suffix, and all three together. Each run shuffles a fresh set.
Are the generated names trademark-safe?
No. Generated names are starting points, not legal clearance. Always search your trademark office database and check domain availability before using a name commercially.
Can I use these names for my company?
Yes, the ideas are free to use, but a name being generated here does not mean it is available. Do your own availability and trademark checks first.
Why do some names repeat between runs?
The prefix and suffix lists are finite, so with a short keyword the best combinations can reappear. Try a different keyword or industry for a wider spread.
Is my keyword uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything is generated in your browser with JavaScript. Your keyword never leaves your device.
