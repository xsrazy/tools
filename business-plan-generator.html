Why start with an outline?

A full business plan can wait. An outline forces you to answer the six questions that matter: what you sell, who buys it, why they pick you, how money comes in, how they find you, and what you do first. If you cannot answer those in a paragraph each, the idea is not ready.

After the outline

  • Turn the market section into real numbers: size, growth, competitors.
  • Price your product and model 12 months of cash flow.
  • Talk to 10 potential customers before writing another word.
  • Use the outline as the skeleton of a pitch deck or loan application.

Business Plan Outline Generator FAQs

Is this a complete business plan?
No. It generates a structured outline from your inputs: a starting skeleton with your own facts filled in. A bank-ready plan still needs financial projections, market data and detail you add yourself.
Who is this outline for?
Founders validating an idea, students, and anyone who needs a clear one-page structure before writing a full plan or pitch deck.
Can I edit the generated outline?
Yes. Copy it into any document editor and expand each section. The outline is a starting point, not a finished document.
Do I need to create an account?
No. There is no account, no sign-up and no server. Everything happens in your browser.
Is my business idea uploaded anywhere?
No. Your inputs never leave your device. The outline is generated locally with JavaScript.
