How the names are built

Each country ships with lists of common first and last names, so combinations feel realistic instead of random gibberish. The generator pairs one first name with one last name, then builds a username from them plus a few random digits, and an email on top of that. Everything is random and nothing here corresponds to a real person.

Good uses for fake names

  • App testing: fill sign-up forms and user tables with plausible data.
  • Design mockups: realistic names beat Lorem Ipsum in wireframes.
  • Privacy: use a placeholder name in demos and screenshots.
  • Writing: quick character names with a believable national flavor.

The suggested emails use example.com, which is reserved for examples and never reaches a real inbox. Do not use fake identities for fraud or impersonation.

Fake name generator FAQs

How are the fake names generated?
Each country has lists of common first and last names. The generator pairs them at random, then builds a username from the name plus random digits, and an email address on top of the username.
Which countries are supported?
Ten: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, Spain, India, Japan and Brazil.
Are these real people?
No. The combinations are random and assembled from public name lists. Any resemblance to a real person is pure coincidence.
Can I use the suggested email addresses?
They are built on example.com, which is reserved and never delivers mail. They are meant as placeholders for tests and mockups, not real inboxes.
Is anything uploaded or saved?
No. Generation happens entirely in your browser and nothing leaves your device.
