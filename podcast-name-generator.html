How the names are built

The generator takes your keyword, title-cases it, then mixes it with descriptive adjectives and classic podcast suffixes like Show, Hour, Talks and Sessions. Each topic adds its own flavor words: tech names lean digital, true crime names lean dark, comedy names lean silly. Six pattern styles keep the batch varied instead of repeating the same formula.

Picking a great podcast name

  • Keep it short: two to four words are easiest to remember and search.
  • Say it out loud: if it is hard to spell after hearing it once, keep generating.
  • Check availability: search podcast directories for your favorite before committing.
  • Match the tone: the topic selector steers names toward your show's vibe.

Podcast name generator FAQs

How does the podcast name generator work?
Type a keyword, choose a topic, and the generator combines your keyword with adjectives and suffixes like Show, Talks and Sessions in six different patterns. Every batch is unique.
What topics are supported?
General, Tech, True Crime, Comedy, Business, Health, Sports and History. Each topic adds themed adjectives to the mix so names match your show's tone.
How many names do I get per batch?
Ten fresh ideas per click. Hit Regenerate as many times as you want; duplicates are filtered out within each batch.
Can I use these names for my show?
The names are generated from common words and patterns, but always search podcast directories and trademark databases before settling on one, since availability varies.
Is anything uploaded or saved?
No. The whole tool runs in your browser, so your keyword never leaves your device.
