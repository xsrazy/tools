How Unicode fonts work

These are not real fonts, they are special Unicode characters that look like styled letters: bold serif, script, double-struck, circled and more. Because the output is plain text, it pastes into Instagram bios, TikTok captions, X posts and WhatsApp with no app or install needed. If a character shows as a box, your device font simply does not include it.

Tips for stylish text

  • Bios love bold: bold serif and sans bold stay readable at small sizes.
  • Script for flair: script styles suit names and short phrases, not long paragraphs.
  • Mix sparingly: one or two styles per bio keeps it clean.
  • Test first: preview the style on the app where you will use it before posting.

Font generator FAQs

How does the font generator work?
It swaps your letters for lookalike characters from Unicode's mathematical symbol blocks. The result is plain text, so it pastes anywhere normal text works.
Will these fonts work on Instagram and TikTok?
Yes. Bios, captions and comments on Instagram, TikTok, X and most apps accept Unicode text, so the styled text shows up without installing anything.
Why do some styles show as boxes?
Boxes mean your device's font does not include that Unicode character. Most modern phones and browsers render all 16 styles correctly.
How do I copy a style?
Tap or click any style row and it is copied to your clipboard instantly, ready to paste into your bio, caption or post.
Is my text uploaded anywhere?
No. The styles are generated in your browser with JavaScript. Your text never leaves your device.
