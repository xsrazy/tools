Why HEIC needs converting

HEIC is the default photo format on iPhones since iOS 11. It keeps great quality in small files, but many Windows programs, websites, printers and older phones cannot open it. JPG opens everywhere, so converting makes your photos universally usable.

HEIC to JPG FAQs

What is HEIC?
HEIC is the photo format iPhones use by default since iOS 11. It stores high quality in small files, but many Windows apps, websites and older devices cannot open it, which is why converting to JPG helps.
Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. Conversion runs entirely in your browser with a local decoding library. Your photos never leave your device.
Will converting to JPG lose quality?
A tiny amount. JPG is a lossy format, but at the default 92% quality the difference is invisible in normal viewing while the file works everywhere.
Can I convert multiple HEIC files at once?
Yes. Select or drop several HEIC files and each one converts separately with its own download button, plus a download-all option.
Does this work on an iPhone?
Yes. Open this page in Safari or Chrome on your iPhone, pick HEIC photos from your library, and download the JPG versions.
