    How the wheel works

    Type your entries and press SPIN. The wheel spins several full turns with realistic slowdown physics and lands on one slice. The pointer at the top marks the winner.

    Use Remove winner to take a picked entry off the wheel, handy for raffles or drawing names one by one. Shuffle randomizes the order of the slices without removing anything.

    Spin the wheel FAQs

    Is the spin the wheel result truly random?
    Yes. Each spin adds a random number of full turns plus a random offset, and the easing animation only affects how it slows down. Every entry always has an equal chance.
    Can I use this as a yes or no wheel?
    Yes. Tap the Yes / No preset to load those two choices, then spin. It works for any decision with two or more options.
    How many entries can I add?
    You can add up to 100 entries. The wheel redraws automatically and each slice gets an equal share.
    Can I remove the winner and spin again?
    Yes. After a spin, use the Remove winner button to take the winner off the wheel, then spin again for the next pick.
    Is my list uploaded anywhere?
    No. Entries stay in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server.
